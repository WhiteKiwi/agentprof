# AgentProf — design execution contract

Canonical rationale and status: [docs/DESIGN-GUIDELINES.md](docs/DESIGN-GUIDELINES.md). Candidate 0.1; reusable scaffold does not ship the profiler.

- Quiet technical instrument panel; charcoal and restrained ember identity from the supplied “FOLLOW THE SLOW.” reference. Use local system type and tabular numerals.
- Semantic `--ap-*` roles from `design/tokens.css`; pair brand orange with dark text. Success/error/unknown have distinct text and status roles. Do not hardcode palette choices in components.
- Prioritize observed time, coverage and useful next actions. Unknown/unsupported is not 0. Slow Tool is not automatically waste. Every synthetic demonstration is visibly labeled.
- Native buttons, labels, selects, details and table headers; visible keyboard focus; project touch target 44px. Rest/hover/active/focus/disabled and error/selected states in both themes.
- Recompose at 320px without losing evidence; wrap Korean and long IDs. Compact brand, no glowing analytical UI, no automatic animation. Respect reduced motion and print.
- Offline report assets are local/inlined. No remote fonts/CDN/fetch/telemetry. No log content or HTML injection through dynamic strings.
- Preserve supplied originals and provisional-vs-final distinction. New logo artwork requires a deliberate separate choice.
- Before committing design changes: build/static checks, contrast pairs, rendered dark/light narrow/wide, keyboard and offline QA; update guideline and [QA evidence](docs/DESIGN-QA.md) together.

Shared WhiteKiwi tone: follow [Kiwi/PIP](https://github.com/WhiteKiwi/kiwi-design-system) neutral/editorial discipline, one accent protagonist and static-vs-interactive distinction. Keep AgentProf's explicitly requested ember/salamander identity. See guideline for bounded product exceptions.
